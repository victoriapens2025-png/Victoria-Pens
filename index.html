Artisan Philosophy

No Room for Boring Nibs

Some people like to write a ceratian way, and they want their nibs to match just that angle, just that style, while some people like trying different writing styles, and often change their natural font & angle to "work better" with the chosen nib. whether the former or the latter, my only goal is to make you pen make you smile, or atleast convey emmotions when you write.

I've never met a pen that can't be fixed, somehow, or in some way.
